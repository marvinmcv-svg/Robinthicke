Robin Thicke / Story

The story

Self-taught at twelve, writing hits at sixteen, Diamond-certified at thirty-six. Still at the piano.

Robin Alan Thicke was born in Los Angeles in 1977, the son of actors Alan Thicke and Gloria Loring. He taught himself piano at twelve, and by sixteen he was writing and producing for R&B acts, first through Brian McKnight and then for Brandy, Color Me Badd, 3T and more.

By twenty-one his songs were on more than twenty gold and platinum albums, for Michael Jackson, Usher, Christina Aguilera, Pink and Jennifer Hudson. Then he stepped out from behind the board.

The Evolution of Robin Thicke (2006) changed everything: “Lost Without U” spent eleven weeks at No. 1 on Billboard’s R&B chart. Seven years later “Blurred Lines” topped the Hot 100 for twelve weeks and went Diamond. Since 2019 he has sat on the panel of FOX’s The Masked Singer, and in 2026 he became band leader on Name That Tune.
