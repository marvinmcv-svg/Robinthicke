Accessibility

Our commitment to making this website usable by everyone.

We want everyone to be able to use this Site, and we aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

What we've done

  • If your device is set to reduce motion, the Site turns off the 3D hero, smooth scrolling, looping animations and page transitions.
  • Every control can be reached with a keyboard and shows a visible focus outline. Dialogs close with Escape.
  • Images have text alternatives, and decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text and controls are designed for strong contrast on the dark background.
  • Song previews can be paused at any time, and nothing plays sound until you press play.

Known limitations

  • Players and posts embedded from Spotify, YouTube and Instagram are made by those companies, and we can't fully control their accessibility.
  • Some decorative animations (the rotating discography and the countdown digits) are visual only. The same information is available as text on the Music and Tour pages.

Feedback

If something on the Site doesn't work for you, tell us at privacy@your-domain.com, and include the page and what you were trying to do. We'll try to reply within five business days and to give you the information in another way in the meantime.
