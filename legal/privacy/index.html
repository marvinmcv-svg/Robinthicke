3. Marvin, the AI receptionist

Marvin runs in your browser. For simple questions it answers from information already on the Site, and nothing leaves your browser. When the AI service is switched on, the text of your recent messages is sent from our server to Anthropic, PBC, which provides the Claude AI model, to generate a reply. We don't attach your name or email to these requests and we don't keep chat transcripts; Anthropic processes them under its commercial terms and does not use them to train its models. Booking details you give Marvin stay in your browser and are only sent if you choose to send the email draft yourself. Please don't share sensitive information with Marvin.

4. Other companies' services on the Site

Some parts of the Site come from other companies' servers. When they load, your browser connects directly to that company, which receives your IP address and may set its own cookies:

  • Instagram (Meta Platforms): the post previews, only if you allow embeds in the cookie banner.
  • Spotify: song previews and players, when you press play or open a player.
  • YouTube (Google): videos, only when you press play, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.
  • Wikimedia Commons: some photos on the Story page.
  • Google Fonts: the typefaces used on the Site.
  • Vercel: hosting.

Their own privacy policies apply to that data. Links to ticket sellers, the store and social networks take you to their sites, which have their own policies.

5. Sharing and selling

We don't sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). We share it only with the service providers above, as needed to run the Site, or if the law requires it.

6. International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where data from the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland is transferred, the providers rely on safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.
